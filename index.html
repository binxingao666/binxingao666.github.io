I am a PhD student at University of Maryland. I study the economics of AI and innovation.

My research explores how openness in AI development shapes competitive dynamics and market structure. Part of my work is funded by OpenAI Researcher Access Program.

Previously, I received my B.Sc. in Finance and Statistics from Peking University Guanghua School of Management, and my M.Sc. in Financial Economics from Columbia Business School.
