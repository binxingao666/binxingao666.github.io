Working Papers

Working Paper OpenAI Research Access Program

OpenAI or CloseAI? That is A Question: A Model & Some Empirical Evidences

Binxin Gao

This paper investigates how the degree of openness in AI development affects innovation dynamics, competitive structure, and social welfare. Using a theoretical model combined with empirical analysis, I examine the trade-offs between open-source and proprietary approaches in the AI industry.

Mentors: Prof. Serguey Braguinsky, Prof. Evan Starr

Work in Progress

IP to IPO: Patent Purchases and the Market for Window Dressing

Binxin Gao

This project examines the strategic acquisition of patents by firms approaching IPO, investigating whether patent purchases serve as substantive innovation signals or window dressing for public market debut.
