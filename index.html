Buy to Build or Buy to Bluff? Patent Acquisitions and Startup IPO Performance

Gao, B.

Working Paper

Using 1,007 VC-backed IPOs merged with USPTO patent assignments, I find that startups purchasing patents in the 12 months before IPO experience 10 percentage points lower first-day returns than non-buyers. Exploiting within-firm timing variation, I show markets penalize only last-minute acquisitions while rewarding gradual accumulation, with a 10% increase in pre-IPO purchases reducing closing prices by 0.8%. These results suggest sophisticated investors distinguish genuine capability building from window dressing based on acquisition timing.
